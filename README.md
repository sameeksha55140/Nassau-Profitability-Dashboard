Nassau Candy Distributor – Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing product-level profitability and margin performance for Nassau Candy Distributor using data analytics and interactive business intelligence dashboards.

Live demo 🌐
https://sameeksha55140-nassau-profitability-dashboard-app-zviy9v.streamlit.app/

The objective of the project is to identify:

* High-profit product lines
* Low-margin risk products
* Revenue vs profitability imbalance
* Division-level financial performance
* Profit concentration and dependency risks

The project transforms raw sales and cost data into actionable business insights that support pricing strategy, product optimization, and profitability-driven decision-making.

---

 🎯 Problem Statement

For distributors like Nassau Candy, high sales volume does not always indicate high profitability.

Some products:

* Generate high revenue but low profit
* Carry excessive operational or manufacturing cost
* Reduce overall company margins despite strong sales

Without proper profitability analysis, pricing and portfolio decisions become reactive rather than data-driven.

This project solves that challenge through interactive analytics and visual dashboards.

---

 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze product-level profitability
* Measure gross margin performance
* Compare division-wise revenue and profit
* Detect low-margin and high-risk products
* Identify products driving the majority of profit
* Support strategic pricing and sourcing decisions
